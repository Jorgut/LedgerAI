## Description
HENG is a private, local-first bookkeeping app. No sign-up, no HENG account, and your ledger stays on your iPhone by default.

**Why HENG**
- No account and no developer server
- Scan receipts, speak an entry, or type it manually
- Budgets, category reports, and a 6-month trend
- Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Russian
- Multiple themes, dark mode, and large text
- Export CSV / JSON anytime
- Optional iCloud sync writes only to your own CloudKit private database

**Privacy**
OCR, speech recognition, and insights run on-device. The developer does not collect, store, or transmit your ledger. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. If you enable iCloud sync, data stays in your own iCloud.

**Who it is for**
People who want to track everyday spending without creating an account or handing their ledger to a third-party server.
