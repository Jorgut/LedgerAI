## Description
HENG is a private, local-first bookkeeping app for people who want to know where their money goes without handing their data to a server.

**Why HENG**
- No account, no login, no cloud
- Works offline — even in airplane mode
- No ads, no pop-ups, no subscription
- Your data stays on your iPhone; export CSV / JSON anytime
- Receipt OCR and voice entry run on-device
- Budgets and monthly reports designed to be read in seconds

**What you can do**
- Snap a receipt: amount, merchant and category are filled in
- Say “lunch 12 dollars” and save it as an expense
- Set a monthly budget and category budgets
- See monthly trends, category breakdowns and local insights
- Export your ledger to CSV or JSON; import a backup
- Large type and high-contrast options for parents and low-vision users

**Privacy by design**
HENG does not create accounts and does not upload your ledger. OCR and speech recognition run on your device. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected.” iCloud sync is optional and only uses your own private iCloud database.

**Pricing**
Free to download with the core bookkeeping experience. A one-time Pro unlock is planned for 1.1 — no subscription.
